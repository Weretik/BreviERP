The real byte roundtrip verifies length/SHA-256 and matching PDF/JPEG/PNG signatures. Authorization happens before any byte call through IPrivateFileAccess. Tests deny a foreign actor with known file IDs and a foreign configuration ID. The adapter rejects public/broad-permission roots and symbolic/reparse paths. Limits are 20 MiB inclusive and 30 committed files under an exclusive filesystem lock. There is no destructive retention API.
