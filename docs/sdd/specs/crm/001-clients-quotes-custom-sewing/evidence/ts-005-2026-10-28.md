## Behavior and contract

Canonical source: `docs/sdd/contracts/crm/quote-drafts.openapi.yaml`, referenced from aggregate OpenAPI. New operations: getCrmQuoteVersions, getCrmQuoteVersion, getCrmQuoteEvents, getCrmQuoteAssignees, repriceCrmQuote, copyCrmQuote, transferCrmQuote, setCrmQuoteArchived.

- History returns paged version/action/parent/author/time and typed historical snapshots, without repricing. Events separately expose revision, action, actor, previous/current owner and reason under current quote scope.
- Explicit reprice uses the existing per-variant previews for old/new comparison. Confirmation names the exact current version/revision and a fresh actor/quote/intent-bound token for every variant. It accepts no replacement content or totals. Missing/expired/mismatched tokens, changed source/revision and blocked/null prices fail atomically.
- Copy selects an authorized saved version, deep-copies its saved prices without a Catalog call, starts a fresh root/draft and logical IDs, records source quote/version, and inherits no number, issued pointer or delivery events. Human numbering remains behind EN-005.
- Transfer validates an unlocked eligible Manager/Admin through a Host adapter, changes owner and appends audit in the same transaction. Former Managers lose all reads/history and replay access. The initiating response contains only the snapshot they were authorized to transfer; subsequent replay after loss is 404.
- Archive/restore is revision-checked and reversible without deleting saved versions. Ready/sent edits become draft and preserve a same-quote issued-version pointer. Actual ready/send endpoints remain later tasks.
- SQL quote filters/counts agree on search/state/archive/owner, inclusive createdFrom, exclusive createdBefore and sourceQuoteId; date offsets normalize to UTC. Assignee search is bounded/paged by name, excludes locked/noneligible users and returns no emails/authentication data.
